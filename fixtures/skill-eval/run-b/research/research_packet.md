# Research packet — Run B

## Question

Why can total AI electricity demand rise while energy per task falls?

## Evidence

- IEA projects global data-center use rising from 485 TWh in 2025 to 950 TWh in 2030. `[fact-global-demand]`
- Berkeley Lab models a U.S. 2030 range of 521–843 TWh rather than one certain value. `[fact-us-range]`
- IEA reports rapid per-task efficiency gains, but also a shift toward much more energy-intensive AI uses. `[fact-efficiency-tension]`

## Interpretation

Efficiency is one variable in a three-part system: efficiency, adoption, and task intensity. Forecasts differ because small changes in those variables compound across millions of users and new facilities.

## Counterpoint and uncertainty

Efficiency and infrastructure bottlenecks can restrain growth. A credible argument should explain why the central case rises without presenting the high case as inevitable.

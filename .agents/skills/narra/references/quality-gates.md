# Editorial quality gates

Apply the checklist for the requested stage. A failed critical item blocks the next creative gate.

## Research

- Open every cited page; do not rely on search snippets.
- Prefer primary, official, or academic evidence for material claims.
- Record publisher, title, URL, access date, publication date when available, and source type.
- Give every important fact at least one valid source ID.
- Preserve scope, units, baseline year, scenario, and forecast uncertainty.
- Include at least one meaningful counterpoint or limiting condition.
- Flag conflicts between sources instead of silently selecting one.
- Distinguish observed data from projections and inference.

## Thesis

- State an argument, not merely a topic.
- Make the argument provable from recorded facts.
- Explain why the evidence changes the viewer's understanding.
- Address the strongest counterpoint without overstating certainty.
- Reject candidates that require unsupported causality or prediction.
- Present 2–3 candidates and stop for creator selection.

## Script

- Open with the central tension, not generic scene-setting.
- Ensure each material claim maps to fact IDs and sources.
- Mark unsupported or ambiguous claims `NEEDS_REVIEW`; never hide them in narration.
- Build a causal or explanatory progression rather than a fact list.
- Avoid repeated setup, inflated stakes, false certainty, and invented quotations.
- Keep narration near the target duration using an explicit speaking-rate assumption.
- Make the conclusion resolve the thesis while retaining genuine uncertainty.

## Storyboard

- Cover every narration passage and material claim.
- Give each shot a visual purpose; reject decorative random B-roll.
- Use evidence/document visuals for claims that benefit from direct proof.
- Use charts/maps only when they clarify a relationship or geography.
- Split scenes into multiple shots when pacing or visual logic changes.
- Ensure per-scene shot duration totals align with narration duration.
- Choose an explicit asset route and mark evidence requirements.
- Keep AI-generated imagery illustrative; do not present it as documentary evidence.

## Review output

Report findings in severity order with artifact path and item ID. Separate confirmed contract/provenance errors from creative preferences. State what was not checked. Do not edit when the user requests review only.
